Teaching Points

  1. One infrastructure failure affects all users simultaneously
  2. Users don't know they share infrastructure until it fails
  3. Failure cascades based on service dependencies
For the Presenter Add 5-8 users, let them "perform activities" for a moment, then click "Trigger Cascade" to show how a single infrastructure failure affects everyone at once. Share the user link to let people experience being affected by failures they can't control.
